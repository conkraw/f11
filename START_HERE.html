PICU Handoff 6.12

One latest blood gas, labeled VEN, ART or CAP, with its collection date and time.

Install this update

  1. Save open work and extract PICU612.zip.
  2. Open PICU_Handoff_v6_12_Latest_Gas and upload its contents to your existing code repository. Replace matching files, include blood_gases.py, and keep app.py at the root.
  3. Keep your current Streamlit Secrets, password hash, encryption key, token and private data repository. Do not run the secrets generator again.
  4. Restart the deployment and look for Version 6.12 | Latest gas: VEN / ART / CAP.

No new dependencies. Earlier archives migrate to schema 12 using the existing key. Existing medication mappings and infusion-display rules are preserved. Do not downgrade individual files after saving the new schema.

Use your new P section

Keep the paired p dividers around the full Epic gas table, including its Lab/Units header, dates and times. The wrapped header in your new format is supported. No extra heading is required. Paste the full SmartPhrase or P alone, then click Create / update handoff.

The Lab Results / Trends area displays one horizontal gas summary. The newest specimen/column with explicit results wins, regardless of whether it is venous, arterial or capillary. Empty cells, -- and < > are omitted. Values from different columns or specimens are never combined. A newer partial gas is not filled from an older complete one.

A P update replaces the old P snapshot. An empty P clears it. Omitting P preserves it. Once P is present, old G gas rows cannot override it. Legacy G-only handoffs show a qualified latest dated specimen group until P is supplied.

Review and edit

Open Labs / Vitals, then Latest blood gas. Older columns remain under Review retained gas columns but are not printed. You can edit collection dates, recorded values and units, or exclude a gas. Unresolved date-only or exact-time ties use source order with a visible qualifier.
