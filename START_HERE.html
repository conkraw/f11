Recent laboratory results and a separate suggested-problem review band.

Install

Save your open work. Extract PICU630.zip, open PICU_Handoff_v6_30_Problem_Review, and upload its contents into the existing code repository. Keep app.py at the root. Keep all current Secrets, both passwords, encryption key, token, public BMI reference and private data repository. Do not run the generators. Restart the deployment and refresh your browser.

Laboratory display

Lactate, theophylline and CK use the last 24 hours. Date-only results overlapping the cutoff are visibly qualified. CRP and procalcitonin are limited to admission onward. NT-proBNP uses the latest result from all supplied dates. Raw results remain editable. Downloaded files do not update themselves, so download again for refreshed filtering.

Suggested problems

Open Suggested problems. Review each trigger, edit its wording, confirm it or dismiss it. You can hide the separate printed band. These prompts never rewrite source Diagnoses. Antibiotic mapping, support devices, low serum CO2 and bowel-regimen use do not independently establish the named diagnoses. Kidney review requires a rise, not just a difference.

An abnormal asterisk does not distinguish low from high. Optional patient-specific low limits require clinician review and matching source units. No universal pediatric limits are assumed.
