PICU Handoff 6.11

Removable custom rooms, consistent preset room names, and neurologic infusions with bold medication names and emphasized doses.

Install this update

  1. Save open work. Extract PICU611.zip.
  2. Open PICU_Handoff_v6_11_Rooms_Infusions and upload its contents into your existing application code repository. Replace matching files and keep app.py at the root. Include infusions.py.
  3. Keep your existing Streamlit Secrets, password hash, encryption key, token and private data repository. Do not run the secrets generator again.
  4. Restart the deployment. Look for Version 6.11 | Custom rooms and neurologic infusions.

No new dependencies. Saved archives upgrade to schema 11 using the existing key. Do not downgrade individual files after the next save.

Room controls

All preset rooms begin with P. Previously saved numeric labels such as 4201 become P4201 without changing the handoff ID or content.

In an added room, expand Custom room options and click Remove custom room. Admin also has a Custom rooms section. Removal takes the custom room and its current handoff out of the active archive and Print all. Medication rules are unchanged.

Undo last room removal restores the last removed room during this session. Failed saves leave the room intact. Earlier encrypted Git history is not erased.

Neuro infusion display

Vital signs and neurologic observations remain first. Infusions follow in their own group. Medication/formulation names are bold, and explicit ordered doses are bold and underlined. A different recorded pump rate appears separately. Notes and other medications follow.

Automatic detection uses continuous parenteral orders. PRN infusion-syringe products are not classified by product name alone. Beside a neurologic order, Neuro display lets you choose Automatic, Infusion, or Other medication. That choice is remembered for its formulation, route and frequency, independently of its system mapping.

Admin > Infusion display choices lets you edit an override without an open handoff. Automatic clears the override. No dose or clinical setting is inferred.

Open the synthetic PDF preview or open the HTML preview.

What remains unchanged

Autosave before room changes, manual saving, compact landscape Print all, lab trees, vitals, cultures, positive viruses, weight/dosing weight, admission date, respiratory settings and medication hiding remain included. No sample-loading buttons return.
