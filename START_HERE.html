Version 6.5 | Cultures and ADS medication fix

Existing archives migrate to schema 8 without replacing the key or existing fields. Save only after all application files have been updated. Do not downgrade part of the application after saving.

Medication fix

Empty TAB cells at the end of a final ADS Override Pull entry are now retained. A clearly labelled name-only ADS entry is also accepted when the clipboard has already removed its empty cells. Repeated entries remain separate. Missing doses, routes and frequencies are not inferred. Hide or restore entries using the existing medication controls.

Cultures

Paste the F block with its table tabs. The app imports the collection date, sample location and organism/result. Sensitivity columns are omitted. Additional organism rows beneath a dated specimen are kept with that specimen.

Temperature remains at the top of ID, followed by a six-dash separator, the culture summary, and then notes and medications.

The compact summary selects the newest two date/specimen groups per category: blood, respiratory, urine and other sites. Repeated same-date/site rows combine for display only, keeping their distinct organisms and statuses. They are not assumed to be one culture. All source rows remain available in the new Cultures tab. An unspecified aspirate is not assumed respiratory.

Use Cultures to edit dates, locations and organisms or exclude a row. An F-only paste updates cultures without replacing the other sections. An included empty F block clears old cultures. Omitting F leaves them unchanged.
