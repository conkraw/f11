New groups

ColumnDisplay
NEUROInfusions, PRN, Paralytic PRN, Fever Control
CARDIACCV Infusions, with bold names and bold, underlined doses
HEMEDVT Prophylaxis for enoxaparin, cangrelor and heparin with an explicit Units/kg/hr Dose
FEN / GIDiuretics, alongside the existing GI prophylaxis and Bowel Regimen groups

These are your display groups, not clinical indications or a determination that a dose is prophylactic. Flushes and heparin mL/hr carriers are not selected by product strength. PRN status comes from frequency, not the medication name. Hidden entries stay hidden, and no order is duplicated by grouping.

Calcium

CA is recognized as calcium and displayed beside Mg and Phos beneath each BMP tree with its own date and flags. Missing results stay missing. No corrected calcium is calculated.

Adjusting groups

Use Medication group, FEN/GI display, or the Neuro/CV display control beside an order. Reusable choices are also available in Admin. Changes save with the existing encrypted settings. Automatic defaults apply to already saved medication records without re-pasting.

Vitals remain first in their columns. Intake/Output/Net remain first in FEN/GI. The CBC and blood-gas parsers and drawing code are unchanged. Existing import-recovery warnings and the shared technical error log are preserved.
