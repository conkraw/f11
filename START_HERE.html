Install the complete package

Extract PICU626.zip. Open PICU_Handoff_v6_26_Devices and copy its contents to your existing application code repository. Replace matching files and keep app.py at the root. Include the new devices.py. Restart the deployment and refresh your browser.

Keep your app password, Admin password, encryption key, token, medication rules, and private data repository unchanged. Do not rerun either setup generator. No new dependencies are required. Installing the update does not clear handoffs. Schema 20 adds the device snapshots. After saving, do not downgrade.

Import and review

Choose the room, paste your full SmartPhrase or complete paired U and V sections, and click Create / update handoff. Review Edit handoff → Devices / airway and EVD. Edit or exclude entries there, then preview and save.
