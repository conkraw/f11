PICU HANDOFF / VERSION 6.15

Intake/output and FEN/GI medication groups

Install the update

Save your open work. Extract PICU615.zip and open PICU_Handoff_v6_15_FEN_GI. Upload all of its contents to your existing application code repository, replacing matching files. Keep app.py at the root. Include the new fluid_balance.py and fen_gi.py. Restart the deployment.

Keep your current Streamlit Secrets, password, encryption key, token and private data repository unchanged. Do not run the secrets generator. No new dependencies are required.

Intake, Output and Net first

Paste the full SmartPhrase or a complete paired S section, then click Create / update handoff. For example:

ssssssssssssssssssssssss
Intake/Output Summary (Last 24 hours) at 1/7/2026 0614
Last data filed at 1/7/2026 0500
Gross per 24 hour
Intake    1200.5 ml
Output    950 ml
Net       250.5 ml
ssssssssssssssssssssssss

These totals print at the very top of FEN/GI. They are copied, not calculated. H is not added to S. Missing values stay missing. Edit them under Edit handoff > FEN / GI.

GI prophylaxis and Bowel Regimen

Famotidine, omeprazole and pantoprazole group automatically under GI prophylaxis. Docusate, lactulose, polyethylene glycol, glycerin suppositories and sennosides group under Bowel Regimen. Each group is separated from the others. Hidden entries stay hidden and distinct orders stay separate.

Medications has a FEN/GI display choice for corrections. Overrides are remembered and can be changed in Admin > FEN/GI display choices. Choosing a different system disables automatic GI grouping for that formulation, route and frequency. Source doses and your saved system mapping are not deleted.

Preserving your handoffs

Omitting S preserves its saved totals. Including an empty S clears them. Other parsers and separate notes are unchanged. Earlier encrypted archives migrate with the same key. After the first schema-15 save, do not downgrade individual application files.

Room-change autosave and Print all include the new display. Long handoffs still use continuation pages rather than clipping content.

Practice and validation

Use examples/synthetic_fen_gi.txt for a test. No sample buttons were added. Open the synthetic preview PDF. See TEST_REPORT.md for executed tests and limits. The hosted Streamlit interface and private data connection were not tested here.
