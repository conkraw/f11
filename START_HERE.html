Demographics

I supplies measured weight and the explicitly labelled dosing weight. N supplies admission date. Only supplied values are used.

Admission is at the top of Patient. Below Diagnoses: weight, dosing weight, then the measured-weight Z-score when provided. Edit them in Labs / Vitals. Percentiles, growth notes and relative entry ages are omitted.

Labs

GLUCOSE fills the BMP tree. MG and PHOS appear underneath each of the two BMP trees. ALBUMIN stays in the horizontal hepatic panel. Dates and source flags remain attached.

The request to put coags in ID is applied literally: CRP/PROCAL and the coagulation group, including Anti-Xa (HEPLMW), are in Infectious Disease below temperature and above cultures, notes and medications. No result is inferred when the source has no HEPLMW row.

What to paste

Paste the full SmartPhrase or complete I, N and G blocks. An included block replaces its own imported snapshot. An empty I clears both imported weights. An empty N clears admission. Omitted sections remain unchanged.
