Update your current app

Save open work. Extract PICU620.zip and open PICU_Handoff_v6_20_Resp_Heme_Infusions. Upload all contents to your existing application-code repository, replacing matching files. Keep app.py at the root. Restart the deployment and refresh your browser.

Keep your current Streamlit Secrets, password, encryption key, token, mappings and private data repository unchanged. Do not run the secrets generator. No additional dependency or storage-format change is needed.

Clearly separated infusion blocks

Respiratory Infusions and Heme Infusions match the existing Neuro and CV style. Medication names are bold. Ordered doses are bold and underlined. A different recorded pump rate stays on a separate line.

Respiratory vitals, support settings and theophylline results remain above the medications. Continuous nebulized/inhaled medicines are included when mapped to Respiratory. Their actual route remains visible. PRN and scheduled treatments stay separate.

Heparin and cangrelor infusions retain their DVT Prophylaxis label inside Heme Infusions. Scheduled enoxaparin remains separate. Orders are not duplicated, and the display heading does not determine a clinical indication.

Change a display choice

The Medications screen now offers Respiratory display and Heme display, with Automatic, Infusion or Other medication. Your choice is remembered using the existing exact formulation, route and frequency. Edit saved choices in Admin > Infusion display choices.

Existing saved handoffs use the new style without re-pasting. Hidden medicines remain hidden. Lab parsing, medication values, save times, room autosave and Print all remain intact.
