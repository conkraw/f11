What changes

Keep this line inside your existing E / VITALS block:

SpO2  Avg: 99.2 %  Min: 93 %  Max: 100 %

The respiratory vital-sign section shows only:

SpO2: 93% to 100%

It appears above support settings, notes and medications in every export. No mapping is needed. Recorded minimum and maximum values are used directly. The average is left out. Other vital signs and ordinary SpO2 reading formats are unchanged.

Updating existing handoffs

Re-paste the full SmartPhrase or full E block. E replaces all previously imported E vitals, so include the other vital signs you still need. It does not replace medications, labs, support settings, manual notes or independent neurologic observations. A paste without E leaves vitals alone. Existing saved summaries are not rewritten on installation.

Existing features

Compact landscape output, horizontal laboratory summaries, CBC/BMP trees, medication taper and PN parsing fixes, hidden medications, neurologic observations, weight/Z-score, respiratory support and Print all remain included.
