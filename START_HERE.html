Update your current app

Save open work. Extract PICU619.zip and open PICU_Handoff_v6_19_Save_Times. Upload all folder contents to your existing app-code repository, replacing matching files. Keep app.py at the root. Restart the deployment, then refresh your browser.

Keep your Streamlit Secrets, password, encryption key, token, mappings and private data repository unchanged. Do not run the secrets generator. No new dependencies or storage-format changes are needed.

In each room

The last successful save date and time appears below the room heading, followed by the current save status. Expand Save status for all rooms to see other preset and custom rooms.

Before printing

Print all shows the most recent handoff save and a per-room list above the download buttons. Unsaved edits remain marked. Click Save all open handoffs to persist them. Downloading a file does not save handoff edits.

Times are Eastern, labeled EST or EDT. They come from confirmed saved handoff records, not the time the page loads or a PDF is generated. Failed saves do not advance the timestamp. Session-only testing is explicitly labeled.

Your team's saves

Use Reload saved archive to see saves made in another session. The display is not a live monitor. A new handoff in a previously used room is labeled not saved yet until it is saved.
