PICU Handoff 6.27

Pacemaker mode and rate in Cardiovascular.

Install the complete package

Extract PICU627.zip. Open PICU_Handoff_v6_27_Pacemaker and copy its contents to your existing application code repository. Replace matching files and keep app.py at the root. Include pacemaker.py. Restart the deployment and refresh your browser.

Keep your app password, Admin password, encryption key, token, medication rules and private data repository unchanged. Do not rerun either setup generator. No new dependencies are required. Installing this update does not clear handoffs. Schema 21 adds the pacemaker snapshot. After saving, do not downgrade.

Import and review

Choose the room, paste the complete SmartPhrase or paired W section, then click Create / update handoff. Your documented AAI mode and atrial rate of 140 BPM produce:

Pacemaker: AAI at 140 bpm

The bold line prints below cardiovascular vital signs and above CV Infusions. No mapping is required. To edit or exclude it, open Edit handoff → Cardiovascular → Edit pacemaker.

Partial updates

Omitting W preserves its previous snapshot. An empty W or Pacemaker No clears it. Missing mode or rate stays UNK. A failed W import leaves the other sections unaffected and hides old automatic pacing settings until reviewed or successfully reimported. The patient's heart rate and pacer output in milliamps are never used as a substitute for the pacing rate.
