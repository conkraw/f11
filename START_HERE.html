PICU HANDOFF / VERSION 6.21

Keep DVT medications together

Heparin and cangrelor infusions remain under a single DVT Prophylaxis heading with enoxaparin and your other assigned medications. Infusion names are bold and ordered doses are bold and underlined. A different recorded pump rate stays separate. These medications do not get an extra Heme Infusions heading.

Unrelated hematology infusions may still use Heme Infusions. Respiratory, neurologic and cardiovascular formatting is unchanged.

Update the application

Save open work. Extract PICU621.zip and open PICU_Handoff_v6_21_DVT_Grouping. Upload its contents to the existing application-code repository, replacing matching files. Keep app.py at the root. Restart the deployment and refresh the browser.

Keep all current Streamlit Secrets, password, encryption key, token, private data repository and mappings unchanged. Do not rerun the secrets generator. No new dependencies or storage-format changes are required.

The sidebar reads Version 6.21 | Unified DVT Prophylaxis group.

Existing handoffs

Saved handoffs use the new grouping when reopened or printed. No re-pasting or remapping is needed. Hidden entries stay hidden, and each visible order appears once. Medication doses, routes, frequencies and stored values do not change.
